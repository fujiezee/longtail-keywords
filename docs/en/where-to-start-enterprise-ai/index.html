Where to start an enterprise AI rollout

Start where work already fails. People cannot find the policy that is in force, or a contract, certificate, or calibration date passes with nobody told. Do not start by hosting a model for the whole company.

One team, ten questions

Write ten sentences people already ask: how leave works, which receipts a claim needs, who calibrated this instrument last. When those ten answer and open the source page, that step is done. If none of them answer, fix the documents before you widen the scope.

Leave the files where they are

Do not announce a move out of the drive and the docs tool. A migration that stops halfway leaves two places to trust. Point the ten questions at the files you already have.

Then add one list of dates

After questions work, add a list: an id, an owner, a date. The owner hears before the date, and the row drops off when the outcome is recorded. Then take a second team.
