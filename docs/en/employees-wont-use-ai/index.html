What if employees will not use the company AI?

They skip it because the first try failed, not because training was short. The answer had no source, or it disagreed with what the team already said in chat, so they asked a person next time. Requiring a login does not change that.

Fix the question they already tried

Take ten questions asked in the team this week. Each one answers and opens the page, or it says clearly that nothing matches. Do not invent a line that sounds like policy. Keep one current copy of each fact.

Attach it to work they already have to do

Besides Q&A, push a contract or certificate due date to the owner. They open it to finish that job, and the questions get a second chance. A demo in an all-hands meeting goes back to zero use.
