How to roll out AI in a company

Rolling out AI in a company means people ask in their own words and get an answer from documents the company already has, with a link to the page, and without seeing files they cannot open where those files live. A chat window that nobody uses for real work is not a rollout.

Connect the documents before you swap the model

Policies sit on a drive, projects sit in the docs people already write, contracts sit somewhere else. The first step is to make those places answerable. A larger model with none of that text still only chats.

Get two jobs working

One is questions: how leave works, how a step is done, when a contract ends. The answer opens the source. The other is dates: a contract, a certificate, or a calibration due date reaches the owner before it lapses, and the outcome is recorded. Without both, the project is still a demo.

Test with a normal employee account

Use ten questions new people actually ask. If there is no answer, fix the document or its title. Pay files and unreleased contracts must come back empty for that login. An admin account can see everything, so it does not count as a test.
