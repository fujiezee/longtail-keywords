Can you use AI without replacing the systems you already have?

Yes. Files stay in the chat-and-docs suite and the drives people already use. AI reads those places. If everyone must move files into a new library first, the move stops halfway and people ask both copies.

Name the places that exist today

Which drive holds policies, where project docs are written, where contracts live. A product that cannot connect to those places does not “support many systems” for you. After it connects, people keep editing the original file, and AI should read the edited one.

Ask from where people already are

They should not install another app to ask one question. A question from the tools they have open returns a link to the original file. Permissions stay with that file: what they cannot open, they cannot be told.

Do not keep a second copy of the text

Pasting the handbook into a new library while the original is still edited makes the two diverge. Index the original. An empty new library is not a rollout.
