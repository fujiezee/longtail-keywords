How a mid-sized company rolls out AI

Do not start by buying a platform for the whole company. The team is small, and the documents already live in the suite and the drives you use. Make those documents answer ten real questions, then add one list of due dates.

You do not need an algorithms team first

Someone names where the files are, which ten questions get asked every week, and which dates keep getting missed. The files stay where they are.

What two weeks should show

Week one: the ten questions answer and open the source, and a normal employee cannot find pay files or unreleased contracts. Week two: an owner hears before a contract or certificate lapses, and the outcome is recorded. Do not widen the scope until both exist.
