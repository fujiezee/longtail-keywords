How to run an enterprise AI pilot

One team, two weeks. Do not start with the whole company. At the end you should be able to name which ten questions were right, which account could not find files it must not see, and which due-date list someone actually finished.

Put only three things in the pilot

Documents that team opens this week. Ten questions in their own words. One kind of due date you already miss: contracts, certificates, or calibration.

What counts as passing

Most of the ten open the source page. A normal employee account finds no pay files or unreleased material. Due items reach the owner before the date. A smooth demo without those three is not a pass.
