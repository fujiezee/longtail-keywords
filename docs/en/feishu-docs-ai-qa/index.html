How to turn Feishu or Lark docs into AI Q&A

Leave the documents in Feishu or Lark. Do not export a second copy into a new library. On a question, retrieve passages that person can already open there, and link the answer to that doc. If they cannot open it in Feishu, it does not appear in the answer.

Index what people ask, not every space

Start with the handbook, policies, work instructions, and live project docs. Skip personal drafts and archived spaces. A title such as “how annual leave works” is easier to hit than “policy compendium 2024.”

Edits in the doc have to show up in answers

People still edit in Feishu. If Q&A froze on the day of an export, it will quote the old clause. After you connect, changes have to be read again.
