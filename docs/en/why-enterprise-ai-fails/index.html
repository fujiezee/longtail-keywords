Why enterprise AI rollouts fail

They fail when the project stops at “the model can chat.” Company documents were never connected, permissions are wider than in the source system, and no daily job depends on it. Everyone tries it on demo day. Two weeks later nobody opens it.

Three places it stalls

The files are still on laptops and in chat threads, so the model cannot read them. A folder only some people can open is searchable by everyone. The only feature is chat, with no contract, certificate, or calibration date that must be handled.

A working rollout is smaller than a launch

Ten real questions in one team answer and open the source page. Files a normal employee cannot open stay hidden. One list of due dates reaches the owner. Widen the scope after those three exist.

Where to start: build an AI assistant on company documents.
